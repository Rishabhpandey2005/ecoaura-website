# 🌱 EcoAura

### Cleaner Homes. Conscious Choices. A Greener Tomorrow.

EcoAura is a sustainable cleaning product concept focused on making
environmentally conscious household cleaning more practical and accessible.

The project explores ideas such as refill-based packaging, reduced plastic
usage, and finding useful applications for suitable organic waste materials.

---

## 💡 The Idea

Everyday household cleaning products often depend on single-use plastic
packaging and conventional formulations.

EcoAura explores a different approach:

- ♻️ Encourage refill-based consumption
- 🌸 Explore useful applications for suitable flower waste
- 🧴 Create practical household cleaning products
- 🌍 Make sustainable choices more accessible
- 🇮🇳 Build the concept around everyday Indian households

---

## 🧴 Product Concept

The current EcoAura concept includes:

### EcoAura Floor Cleaner
An everyday floor-cleaning product concept designed around affordability
and more conscious packaging.

### Flower Waste Cleaner
An exploration of how discarded temple flowers could potentially be
given a useful second life.

### 1L Eco Refill
A refill-oriented format intended to reduce the need for repeatedly
purchasing new containers.

> Note: EcoAura is currently a product and web-development concept.
> The website does not represent a commercially launched product.

---

## ♻️ How It Works

The concept follows a simple four-stage model:

**01 — Collect**  
Collect suitable organic waste materials.

**02 — Transform**  
Explore ways to process and repurpose those materials.

**03 — Create**  
Develop practical household products.

**04 — Refill**  
Encourage reuse and reduce unnecessary packaging.

---

## 🌍 Vision

EcoAura aims to explore how everyday household products can become more
sustainable without making them unnecessarily complicated or expensive.

The long-term vision is to develop practical products that combine
affordability, sustainability and local resource utilisation.
